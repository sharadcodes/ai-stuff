Create a complete PowerPoint presentation with a strict hand-drawn digital notebook aesthetic.

GLOBAL DESIGN RULES

Use a pure AMOLED black background on every slide.

Background color must be exactly #000000 across the entire deck.

Do not use gradients, off-black backgrounds, white slides, light slides, section divider variations, or alternate themes.

Every slide must have the same black background.

FONT RULES

Use only one smooth handwritten font family for the entire presentation.

Use the same handwritten font family for titles, subtitles, body text, labels, captions, diagrams, and notes.

Do not use sans-serif fonts, serif fonts, corporate fonts, decorative fonts, or mixed typography.

Only vary font size, font weight within the same font family, and neon text color.

All text must look smooth, natural, and handwritten.

TITLE RULES

Every slide title must be 1 to 3 words maximum.

Never use a colon in any title.

Do not add subtitles beside or below titles.

Keep titles short, simple, and clear.

Place extra explanation inside the body content, not in the title.

Before finalizing, validate every slide title:

* Maximum 3 words
* No colon character
* No subtitle
* Rewrite any title that violates these rules

Examples of valid titles:

* The Problem
* Key Ideas
* How It Works
* Main Insight
* Next Steps
* Final Thoughts

Examples of invalid titles:

* The Problem: Why It Matters
* Key Ideas for Success
* How It Works in Practice
* Next Steps: What To Do

VISUAL STYLE

Use a polished hand-drawn sketchbook style.

Include hand-drawn arrows, doodles, underlines, marker highlights, sketched diagrams, and simple illustrated icons.

Use slight natural imperfections in lines and shapes to create a real handwritten notebook feeling.

Avoid stock images, corporate templates, glossy 3D visuals, and generic PowerPoint layouts.

Use clean spacing and minimal clutter.

Prefer asymmetrical but balanced layouts.

Use generous margins and readable text sizes.

COLOR SYSTEM

Use neon colors only on the black background:

* Electric blue
* Cyan
* Neon purple
* Magenta
* Lime green

Use colors consistently and sparingly.

Add subtle glow effects to important text, drawings, and highlights.

Maintain strong contrast for readability.

CONTENT STYLE

Keep content modern, creative, simple, and visual.

Use short phrases instead of long paragraphs.

Explain ideas through visual storytelling, diagrams, arrows, callouts, and handwritten notes.

Avoid dense text blocks.

ANIMATION RULES IF SUPPORTED

Use smooth handwriting reveal animations for text.

Use subtle fade-ins for doodles, diagrams, and highlights.

Use only soft transitions.

Do not use aggressive zooms, spins, flashes, or mechanical effects.

STRICTLY PROHIBITED

Do not use any background other than pure black #000000.

Do not change font families anywhere in the deck.

Do not use colons in slide titles.

Do not use titles longer than 3 words.

Do not use subtitles.

Do not use white or light slides.

Do not use corporate templates.

Do not use mixed typography.

Do not use stock photos unless converted into a hand-drawn sketch style.

FINAL OUTPUT GOAL

Create a fully consistent AMOLED black presentation that looks like one continuous premium handwritten digital sketchbook.

Every slide must use the same black background, the same single handwritten font family, short 1 to 3 word titles without colons, neon accent colors, and hand-drawn visual elements.
