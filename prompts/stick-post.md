
Create a tall, vertical social-media one-pager in a minimalist hand-drawn webcomic style.

Use a pure white #FFFFFF background. Draw everything with loose black ink-like lines, rough arrows, uneven boxes, comic panels, handwritten labels, and simple stick figures.

Use only one irregular handwritten font style throughout. All text must look slightly uneven, human-drawn, and informal. No polished typography, sans-serif, serif, corporate fonts, gradients, glossy effects, or stock imagery.

Make the layout vertical and easy to scroll:

Clear top headline area

3–6 simple visual sections stacked downward

One main idea per section

Generous whitespace

Short phrases only

Simple arrows guiding the reader downward

Final takeaway or punchline at the bottom

Every stick figure must have a visible facial expression and expressive body language. Never use blank or neutral faces. Use emotions like confused, curious, skeptical, worried, excited, relieved, or surprised to support the message.

Use mostly black or dark gray ink. Add only tiny muted accents like blue, red, green, yellow, or teal for emphasis.

Keep the tone clever, dry, simple, and explanatory. The result should feel like a hand-drawn comic infographic made for sharing on social media: sparse, readable, funny when appropriate, and visually driven.
